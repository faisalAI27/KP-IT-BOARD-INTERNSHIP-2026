# Landing hero image generation

Audit date: 29 July 2026

## Generation brief

The hero required a photorealistic editorial illustration of Pashtun community
life that makes speaking, listening and intergenerational cultural preservation
immediately visible. Important faces and hands needed to remain in the middle
65% of a 4:3 or 5:4 landscape composition so the scene would survive the
existing arched desktop frame and a tighter mobile crop.

All three candidates were generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation
tool. Every scene and person is fictional.

## Final prompt

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: right-side landing-page hero
> image for KP AWAZ, a community voice preservation project. Candidate B — open
> courtyard community conversation. Create a candid gathering of five fictional
> Pashtun adults in an open traditional courtyard in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. A
> dignified older community member sits slightly forward and speaks naturally
> while younger and middle-aged neighbours listen with quiet attention and warm
> engagement. The visual story is a voice travelling across generations. Use a
> modest warm-stone courtyard with one carved wooden element, a charpai and a
> few restrained woven cushions. Through an open archway, show only a subtle
> soft-focus suggestion of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa mountain foothills and pale sky.
> Architecture and landscape support the human interaction, never dominate it.
> Show fictional individuals with a believable age range, culturally
> appropriate shalwar kameez, waistcoats, shawls and only one or two traditional
> caps, realistically worn. No posing and no direct camera gaze. Use
> photorealistic editorial documentary photography, natural skin texture,
> visible age lines, realistic fabric weave and wear, realistic hands, anatomy
> and sitting positions, and restrained depth of field. Use a high-resolution
> 5:4 landscape, medium-wide eye-level view. Arrange the group in a gentle
> conversational arc. Keep all important faces, the older speaker and gesturing
> hands inside the middle 65% for an arched desktop frame and mobile crop. Use
> soft late-morning mountain light, warm neutral stone, balanced cool shadows,
> natural skin tones, and subtle compatibility with navy, soft teal and muted
> blue. No visible words, logos, signs, flags, seals, watermarks, weapons,
> military or political imagery, protest imagery, religious ceremony, poverty
> framing, war damage, unrelated regional clothing, microphones, headphones,
> smartphones as subject, robots or futuristic technology. Avoid distorted or
> extra fingers, fused hands, duplicate people, asymmetrical eyes, malformed
> teeth or ears, plastic skin, floating objects, impossible furniture,
> inconsistent shadows, excessive orange grading, neon teal lighting,
> fashion-ad posing, tourism-ad gloss and overly symmetrical staging.

## Candidate review

| Candidate | Direction | Review | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Traditional interior Hujra storytelling with five men and a resting rabab | Strong documentary warmth, carved-wood context and clear elder-led conversation. The group is tighter at both edges and offers less representative range than B. Faces and primary hands were credible. | Rejected |
| B | Open courtyard conversation with subtle mountain context and five intergenerational adults | Strongest speaker–listener eyelines, clearest cross-generational story, balanced cultural context, natural expressions and the safest central crop. Full-resolution inspection found believable faces, hands, clothing folds, sitting positions, furniture, background, lighting and shadows, with no text or duplicated objects. | **Selected** |
| C | Intimate four-person Hujra conversation with a rabab beside the group | Emotionally focused and crop-safe, but the rabab neck and peg detailing looked less convincing at full resolution. It was not selected rather than hiding that weakness in a crop. | Rejected |

No regeneration was required: Candidate B passed the full-resolution defect
review and met the crop, cultural, emotional and brand-compatibility criteria.

## Web treatment

The 1,402 × 1,122 PNG master was trimmed by one pixel on every edge to an exact
5:4 composition, then exported as 720 × 576 and 1,440 × 1,152 WebP variants.
The original master is retained for provenance and is not referenced by the
website. A 2,048px derivative was intentionally omitted because the generated
master contains no additional source detail at that size.
