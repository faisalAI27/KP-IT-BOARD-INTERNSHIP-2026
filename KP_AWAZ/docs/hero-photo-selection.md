# Landing hero photo selection

Audit date: 29 July 2026

## Selection criteria

The landing hero needs a real, respectful documentary photograph that connects
Khyber Pakhtunkhwa with people, voice, listening or shared culture. The source
must identify the creator, location or cultural context, reusable licence and
original dimensions. It also needs enough resolution for a focused portrait
crop at desktop density.

## Selected crop

The selected 3:2 original is cropped from its right side to a 4:5 portrait,
keeping the folk singers and mountain setting together. It is exported locally
with a gentle brightness, contrast and colour correction at 720 × 900,
1,440 × 1,800 and 1,920 × 2,400 pixels as WebP. CSS `object-position`
adjustments retain the group at desktop, tablet and mobile breakpoints.
