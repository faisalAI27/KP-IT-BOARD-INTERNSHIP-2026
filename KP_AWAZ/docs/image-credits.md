# Image credits

## Landing-page hero

- **Subject:** Folk singers gathered on a hillside at the Qaqlasht Festival in
  Upper Chitral, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
- **Original title:** “Folk singers at the Qaqlasht Festival, Upper Chitral”
- **Creator:** aeledroos (Wikimedia Commons contributor)
- **Date:** 27 March 2015
- **Source:** [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Folk_singers_at_the_Qaqlasht_Festival,_Upper_Chitral.jpg)
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported
  (CC BY-SA 3.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). The creator
  published the photograph as their own work. The local adaptations are
  distributed under the same license.
- **Local adaptations:** The original 5,184 × 3,456 JPEG was cropped from the
  right side to keep the singers and mountain setting together, then resized
  with a gentle brightness, contrast and colour correction and encoded as
  720 × 900, 1,440 × 1,800 and 1,920 × 2,400 WebP files. No people or scene
  elements were generated, removed or composited. The homepage frame applies
  small responsive `object-fit: cover` display adjustments.
- **Local files:**
  `frontend/assets/images/qaqlasht-folk-singers-720.webp`,
  `frontend/assets/images/qaqlasht-folk-singers-1440.webp` and
  `frontend/assets/images/qaqlasht-folk-singers-1920.webp`
