# Image credits

## Landing-page hero

- **Asset type:** AI-generated illustrative hero image
- **Subject:** A fictional intergenerational Pashtun community conversation in
  a traditional open courtyard, with an elder speaking as others listen
- **Generation tool:** OpenAI image generation tool (built-in image generation
  workflow)
- **Generated:** 29 July 2026
- **People and place:** All depicted people are fictional. The scene illustrates
  Pashtun community life in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa; it is not a record of an actual
  event, named community or documented location.
- **Master:** `docs/source-assets/kp-awaz-cultural-gathering-master.png`
  (1,402 × 1,122 PNG)
- **Processing:** One pixel was trimmed from each edge to normalise the
  composition to an exact 5:4 ratio. The result was resized with Lanczos
  resampling and encoded as WebP. No people or scene elements were added,
  removed or composited, and no colour adjustment was applied.
- **Local web files:**
  `frontend/assets/images/kp-awaz-cultural-gathering-720.webp` (720 × 576) and
  `frontend/assets/images/kp-awaz-cultural-gathering-1440.webp` (1,440 × 1,152)

The candidate review, prompt and selection rationale are recorded in
[`hero-image-generation.md`](hero-image-generation.md).
